💡 HOW IT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Random Forest Regression
   - Ensemble of 100 decision trees
   - Each tree learns different patterns
   - Final prediction = average of all trees
   - Captures complex non-linear relationships

2. Feature Engineering
   - Seasonal encoding (sin/cos of month)
   - Per-capita metrics (kWh per person)
   - Temperature extremes detection
   - Interaction features

3. AI Insights
   - Identifies cost drivers
   - Provides recommendations
   - Explains increase/decrease factors

Factors that INCREASE bills:
    ❌ Extreme temperatures (>85°F or <40°F)
    ❌ Larger homes
    ❌ Higher energy rates
    ❌ More occupants

Factors that DECREASE bills:
    ✅ Moderate weather (~65°F)
    ✅ Efficient homes
    ✅ Lower energy rates
    ✅ Optimal usage patterns
